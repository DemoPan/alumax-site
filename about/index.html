Two Decades of Monumental Cladding & Structural Realization

Founded with the foundational mission to close the gap between daring parametric architectural concepts and industrial-scale manufacturability, ALUMAX has grown from an artisan metallurgical workshop into one of the world's most capable envelope fabrication partners. Over 20+ years, our facade engineering studio has collaborated directly with benchmark global architectural practices—including Foster + Partners, Zaha Hadid Architects, and Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM)—turning complex fluid hyperboloids, extreme wind-shear canopies, and blast-resistant assemblies into pristine, install-ready reality.
