// SECTION 01 Heritage & Manufacturing Realization

Precision Architectural Cladding & Advanced Metallurgy Realization

Established in 2019 with the mission to bridge the gap between daring architectural designs and industrial-scale manufacturability, Aluviona has rapidly developed into an advanced architectural envelope fabrication powerhouse. Anchored by our modern 85,000 m² smart manufacturing campus in Lu'an, Anhui, our engineering and production teams have fabricated and delivered over 5,000,000 m² of precision exterior cladding, solid aluminum cassettes, and bespoke honeycomb envelope assemblies across more than 800 landmark architectural and infrastructure projects nationwide in China.

// SECTION 02 Evolution Timeline

Milestones of Rapid Growth & Engineering Excellence

Chronological progression tracking Aluviona's advancement from factory commissioning in 2019 to over 800 landmark project deliveries and 5M+ m² of architectural envelopes.

2019 — 2021

Campus Commissioning & Heavy Tooling

Establishment of the continuous 85,000 m² smart metal fabrication base in Lu'an Economic Development Zone. Commissioning of 20+ heavy-duty automated production lines, Japanese automated electrostatic coating systems, and 2.3m × 12m continuous honeycomb bonding lines.

Campus Milestone 85,000 m² Built
2022 — 2024

Scale Delivery & Infrastructure Penetration

Rapid scaling across high-speed rail terminals, metro transit networks, regional cultural centers, and university research complexes. Surpassing 3,000,000 m² cumulative fabrication output with certified ASTM & EN compliance.

Scale Milestone 3M+ m² Delivered
2025 — Present

800+ Project Milestone & Next-Gen Smart Facades

Surpassing 5,000,000 m² of delivered architectural envelopes across 800+ projects nationwide. Deep integration of motorized kinetic solar shading systems, parametric CAD-to-CNC LOD 400 fabrication pipelines, and robotic stretch-forming.
