The Product

What are Roll-Coated Aluminum Panels?

Roll-coated aluminum panels (also known as pre-coated, color-coated, or pre-finished aluminum panels) are premium architectural cladding panels fabricated from solid aluminum sheet pre-treated with factory-applied coatings and thermally cured before panel formation. By applying the coating in a tightly controlled continuous roller line rather than on-site or post-spray painting, our panels achieve superior coating uniformity, zero orange peel, exceptional color fidelity, and robust long-term durability.

Customization Options

Tailored to Your Project

From flat cut-to-size cladding panels to precision-folded cassette trays and custom perforated feature walls, our pre-finished panels accommodate diverse architectural designs.

Brake-Folded Cassette Panels

Panels are CNC routed and folded into sharp 90° cassette trays on precision press brakes with internal corner gussets and structural stiffeners for large spans.

CNC Perforation & Feature Panels

Precision multi-tool punching and laser routing create acoustic wall panels, architectural sunscreens, and decorative feature walls with clean edges and zero paint chipping.

Curved & Formed Facades

High-ductility coating formulation accommodates 3-roller pyramid bending and curved fascia panels without surface micro-cracking or finish discoloration.

Installation Typologies

Versatile Structural Mounting & Integration Options

Engineered for fast, reliable site installation across diverse facade sub-frames, accommodating thermal expansion and high structural wind loads.

TYPE A // CASSETTE RAINSCREEN

Concealed Hook-On Cassette System

Pre-finished roll-coated panels are CNC routed and folded into cassette trays with perimeter flanges. Panels mount onto horizontal extruded aluminum transoms using concealed structural hooks and brackets, creating an open-joint ventilated rainscreen with hidden fixings.

TYPE B // DIRECT-FASTENED

Open-Joint Ventilated & Face-Fastened System

Panels are secured directly to vertical aluminum or steel sub-framing channels using color-matched, EPDM-gasketed architectural fasteners. Features engineered expansion slots and rear ventilation cavities for optimal pressure equalization and moisture drainage.
