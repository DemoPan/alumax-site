System Architecture // 系统介绍

What is Pre-Insulated Metal Cladding?

Traditional multi-layer field-assembled envelopes rely on sequential site installation of mineral insulation, vapor barriers, air cavity substructures, and exterior metal rainscreens. ALUMAX Series PI-800 transforms this labor-intensive workflow into a factory-bonded, monolithic insulated composite panel engineered to withstand extreme environmental wind loads and thermal cycles.

Exterior Metal Skin

PVDF / FEVE AA3003-H14 aluminum (0.8–1.5mm) or high-tensile galvanized steel (0.5–1.0mm).

Geometry Options // 定制选项

Architectural Panel Facing Profiles & Finishes

Engineered face profiles configured to manipulate building shadows, structural flatness, and aesthetic rhythms. Fluorocarbon coil-coating tested under ASTM G154; custom RAL, Pantone, and metallic sheen matching available.

Flat Architectural Reveal (FR-01)

Ultra-smooth exterior face with 15mm or 25mm shadow recess joints, engineered for monument towers demanding unblemished planar flatness.

Ribbed Linear Rhythm (RL-02)

Longitudinal extruded profile ribs providing dynamic light reflection while substantially boosting span deflection stiffness.

Micro-Fluted Wave (MF-03)

Subtle sinusoidal surface texture that masks structural oil-canning and micro-deflections over ultra-long unbraced spans up to 10 meters.

Embossed Stucco Texture (ET-04)

Mechanical micro-embossed surface creating a soft diffuse sheen, ideal for high-traffic ground podiums and industrial logistics portals.

Installation // 安装方式

Dry-Construction Fastening & Joints

Engineered for swift dry installation without wet plaster or silicone façade caulking. Zero thermal bridge transmission via proprietary nylon-reinforced thermal breaks.

METHOD 01 // SECRET-FIX

Concealed Fastener T&G Interlock

Extended female flange conceals structural self-drilling bi-metal A2/A4 fasteners; each subsequent panel locks over the fixings for a seamless monolithic façade without exposed screw heads. Factory-integrated butyl mastic and PVC isolator strip.

METHOD 02 // THERMAL-BREAK

Sub-Frame Thermal Decoupling Bracket

Heavy-gauge aluminum structural clips interface with the main building structure via 10mm high-density polyamide PA66 GF25 isolator shims, completely halting thermal conductance into interior slabs. Up to 6.2 kN per bracket, ±25mm adjustability in X, Y, Z axes.

METHOD 03 // CORNER & EXPANSION

Mitered Flashing & EPDM Gasket

Prefabricated factory-bent 2.0mm PVDF aluminum corner units ensure sharp 90° architectural corners without site cutting. Complete weatherproofing via dual vulcanized EPDM compression gaskets backed by integrated internal weeping drainage paths.

Standard installation CAD details (DWG) and LOD 400 Revit families available on request.
