The Product

What are Architectural Metal Ceiling Systems?

Architectural metal ceiling systems are bespoke engineered interior and transition envelope solutions crafted from high-grade aluminum and steel alloys. Going far beyond conventional flat drop-in tiles, our systems specialize in flowing wave-form baffles, curved vaulted panels, and three-dimensional sculptural free-form geometries tailored to landmark architectural visions. Every system seamlessly integrates concealed structural suspension carriers, acoustic absorption fleece, climate diffusers, and continuous ribbon linear lighting—manufactured to exact project specifications, radius schedules, and BIM coordinates for airports, transit terminals, cultural pavilions, and corporate headquarters.

Customization Options

Tailored to Your Project

Beyond conventional flat ceilings, our architectural metal ceiling systems are manufactured into a wide variety of flowing curves, wave forms, and geometric configurations tailored to project-specific BIM models.

Wave-Form Baffle & Linear Strip Systems

Continuous sinusoidal wave profiles and variable-depth vertical baffle fins that create an undulating, flowing river-like movement across the ceiling plane. Spacing and blade heights are custom-tuned for optimal sightline screening and acoustics.

Curved & Vaulted Metal Panel Systems

Roll-formed curved and vaulted aluminum panels engineered for continuous cylindrical arches, barrel vaults, and organic curved soffits. Finished with razor-sharp hairline joints and concealed hook-on brackets for smooth, monolithic curvature.

Sculptural & Free-Form Geometric Ceilings

Complex 3D parametric faceted, hyperbolic, and crystalline ceiling canopies fabricated from CNC-machined cassette components. Developed directly from architectural BIM/Rhino models into sequentially numbered, hoist-ready modular cassettes.

Custom Perforated & Illuminated Systems

Precision gradient micro-perforations (0.7mm to 2.5mm) paired with continuous flush-mounted LED light channels. Combines ambient diffuse backlighting with SoundTex acoustic absorption to balance acoustic performance with dramatic optical elegance.

Installation Typologies

Versatile Structural Mounting & Integration Options

Concealed structural suspension components engineered for zero-deflection spans up to 3,600mm, with factory-punched cutouts for continuous HVAC linear diffusers and recessed linear luminaires.

DETAIL 01 // CONCEALED CARRIER

Heavy-Duty C-Channel & Concealed Spring-Tee

0.8mm cold-rolled galvanized steel C-channel primary runners spaced at 1,200mm intervals, interlocked with spring-tee cross profiles providing a 100% concealed, level ceiling plane. Tolerance ±0.5mm, seam reveal 0.0mm.

DETAIL 02 // SEISMIC BRACING

Rigid Seismic Sway Strut & Safety Cables

4-way diagonal high-tensile aircraft safety wire anchoring and compression telescoping struts neutralize vertical uplifting forces during high-velocity HVAC displacement or seismic events. Tensile rating 2.4 kN per drop.

DETAIL 03 // MEP INTERFACE

Flush Linear MEP & Luminaire Trays

Standardized extruded aluminum adapter tracks allow direct mechanical integration of DALI-controlled linear downlights, sprinkler discharge heads, and high-throw linear slot air diffusers. Slot widths 50 / 75 / 100 / 150mm.
