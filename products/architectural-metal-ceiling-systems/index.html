Systems Architecture // 系统介绍

What is the ALU-CL-6000 Ceiling Matrix?

A fully modular suspended ceiling platform combining acoustic baffles, concealed clip-in tiles, linear planks and open-cell grilles on one precision substructure — engineered for zero-deflection spans up to 3,600mm with factory-punched cutouts for HVAC diffusers and recessed luminaires.

Engineered Integration // 龙骨与机电集成

Precision Substructure & Service Interfacing

Concealed structural suspension components engineered for zero-deflection spans up to 3,600mm, with factory-punched cutouts for continuous HVAC linear diffusers and recessed linear luminaires.

Heavy-Duty C-Channel & Spring-Tee (DETAIL 01)

0.8mm cold-rolled galvanized steel C-channel primary runners spaced at 1,200mm intervals, interlocked with spring-tee cross profiles providing a 100% concealed, level ceiling plane. Tolerance ±0.5mm, seam reveal 0.0mm.

Rigid Seismic Sway Strut & Safety Cables (DETAIL 02)

4-way diagonal high-tensile aircraft safety wire anchoring and compression telescoping struts neutralize vertical uplifting forces during seismic events or high-velocity HVAC displacement. Tensile rating 2.4 kN per drop.

Flush Linear MEP & Luminaire Trays (DETAIL 03)

Standardized extruded aluminum adapter tracks allow direct mechanical integration of DALI-controlled linear downlights, sprinkler discharge heads, and high-throw linear slot air diffusers. Slot widths 50 / 75 / 100 / 150mm.
