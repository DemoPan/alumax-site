The Product

What are Architectural Solar Control Louvers?

Architectural Solar Control Louvers (Extruded S-Blade and Aerofoil Sunshade Systems) are high-performance exterior facade elements fabricated from precision-extruded 6063-T6 aluminum alloys. Designed to mount as continuous horizontal or vertical ribbons across curtain walls, mechanical penthouses, and vision glazing, they incorporate specialized aerodynamic water-stop baffles that deflect driving rain while shading interior spaces from harsh solar heat and glare, all while providing unimpeded ventilation airflow for mechanical systems.
