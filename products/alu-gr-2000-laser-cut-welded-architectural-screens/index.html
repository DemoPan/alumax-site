What is the ALU-GR-2000 Screen System?

Unlike static planar louvers, the ALU-GR-2000 multi-layer Mashrabiya acts as an active visual barrier and passive thermal shield. During zenith solar noon (75°+), 4mm-depth structural ribs block 88% of thermal infrared irradiance while maintaining uninterrupted outward sightlines.

Structural Interface // 安装方式

Concealed Subframe & Fixing Details

Precision-engineered extruded aluminum mounting cleats allow three-axis site adjustment (X, Y, Z ±15mm) while mitigating thermal expansion stresses through neoprene-isolated sliders.

TYPE-CFB-01

Concealed Floating Bracket

Interlocking male-female 6063-T6 cleat mechanism fastened into curtain wall transoms or slab edge brackets via M10 A4-80 stainless anchors — 1.8 kN per cleat dead load, ±15mm 3-axis site adjustment.

TYPE-HMF-02

Hinged Sub-Frame for BMU Access

Screen panels swing 95° inward or outward on internal stainless pivot hinges for glazing washing and BMU cradle access — 10,000 operational cycles, dual aircraft wire safety lanyard.

TYPE-TEI-03

Thermal Bushing & Isolators

Dielectric PTFE shims and EPDM rubber collars isolate aluminum frames from carbon steel brackets, eliminating galvanic reaction in high-salinity marine environments — ΔT 60°C (+6.5mm) travel, ASTM D149 tested.
