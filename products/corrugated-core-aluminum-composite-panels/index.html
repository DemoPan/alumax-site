The Product

What are Corrugated Core Aluminum Composite Panels?

Corrugated Core Aluminum Composite Panels (ACCP) are advanced architectural sandwich panels comprising two pre-finished aluminum facing sheets bonded to an internal sinusoidal corrugated wave aluminum core. Unlike traditional composite panels with mineral-filled polymer cores, the engineered wave core creates structural depth and continuous directional support—behaving like an infinite series of micro I-beams. This achieves exceptional flexural strength, high-span rigidity, and non-combustible fire safety at a fraction of the mass of solid metal plate.

layers

Top & Bottom Metal Facings

High-grade architectural AA3003-H14 or AA5052-H32 alloy skins (0.5mm – 1.0mm) factory-finished in multi-coat PVDF, FEVE, or specialized architectural textures.

waves

Sinusoidal Structural Wave Core

Continuous 2.8mm – 6.0mm roll-formed wave aluminum core providing directional shear stiffness, flexural strength, and internal micro-cavity air circulation.

lock

High-Strength Composite Bonding

Continuous thermoset polymer film bonding provides climbing drum peel strength > 98 N·mm/mm, ensuring zero delamination under intense thermal shock.

domain

Architectural Versatility

Engineered for high-rise ventilated rainscreens, unitized curtain wall spandrels, interior cladding, airport terminal ceilings, and soffits.

Key Advantages

Why Choose Our Corrugated Core Aluminum Composite Panels?

Combining the structural efficiency of corrugated geometry with the durability and lightness of architectural aluminum for landmark building projects.

scale

Ultra-Lightweight Construction

Weighing just 4.3 kg/m² for a 4.0mm panel, corrugated composite is over 60% lighter than 3.0mm solid aluminum plate, drastically reducing facade dead loads and simplifying handling.

air

Enhanced Panel Rigidity & Wind Resistance

The corrugated wave core delivers extraordinary flexural rigidity (0.245 × 10¹¹ N·mm²/m), withstanding wind pressures up to ±5.4 kPa (ASTM E330) over 1,500mm spans without intermediate stiffeners.

aspect_ratio

Flawless Optical Flatness

Continuous internal wave support prevents pillowing and thermal warping, ensuring mirror-like optical flatness and crisp reflections across large-format panels up to 1,500 × 6,000mm.

local_fire_department

Certified Non-Combustible Fire Safety

100% metal construction eliminates flammable polyethylene and toxic fumes, achieving Euroclass A2-s1, d0 / Class A1 under EN 13501-1 and ASTM E84 Class A flame spread.

Customization Options

Tailored to Your Project

Engineered for standard architectural fabrication tooling. Panels fold cleanly into rigid cassettes, form smooth curves, and accept custom CNC perforations without outer skin fracturing.

CNC V-Groove Routing & Cassette Return

Routed from the backside through rear skin and core with a 90° carbide cutter; a calibrated 0.3mm base remains to prevent metal fatigue when cold-folding 90° returns. Minimum bend radius R = 2.0mm.

Curved Facades & Column Covers

Directional 3-roller pyramid bending along the flute orientation creates smooth single-radius cylindrical curves, curved fascias, and column wraps without skin wrinkling.

Acoustic & Feature Perforations

High-precision CNC punch tooling and laser routing create acoustic interior ceiling panels and exterior sun-shading screens with clean cutouts and zero core delamination.

Installation Typologies

Versatile Structural Mounting & Integration Options

Engineered for fast, accurate cassette fabrication and concealed sub-frame mounting with dynamic thermal expansion tolerance.

TYPE A // CASSETTE RAINSCREEN

Concealed Hook-On Cassette System (Z-Clip)

Cassette folded return flanges with internal structural corner brackets hook onto continuous vertical aluminum T- or Z-runners with stainless steel isolating fasteners, creating a concealed-fastened open-joint ventilated rainscreen.

TYPE B // MOVEMENT JOINT

Horizontal Shadow Gap Express Joint

Engineered open shadow gaps (15–20mm width) featuring dry-joint EPDM multi-fin weather gaskets or ventilated rainscreen baffles with internal rear weeping drainage, accommodating ±3.5mm thermal movement per panel.
