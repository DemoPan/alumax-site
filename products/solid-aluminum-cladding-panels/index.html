Fabrication & Customization

Fixing Methods & Substructure Details

Three engineered fixing typologies with full CAD elevation detail packages — from pressure-equalized open joints to crane-assisted high-rise cassette erection.

Curved & Double-Curved Cassettes

Precision roll-bent and 3D brake-formed double-curved solid aluminum panels delivering fluid, organic facade transitions with continuous shadow reveals.

Open-Joint Ventilated Rainscreen (TYPE 01)

12mm dry open shadow reveals without exposed silicone; pressure-equalized cavity drains condensate instantly. Substructure: extruded 6063-T6 aluminum mullion.

High-Rise Hook-On Cassette (TYPE 03)

Modular interlocking hung brackets for crane-assisted erection at up to 150m² per team per day; each panel individually demountable with neoprene anti-rattle bushings.

Installation Typologies

Fixing Methods & Substructure Details

Three engineered fixing typologies with CAD elevation detail packages available. All fixing bracket slots feature 6mm ovalized thermal glide tolerances (aluminum linear expansion 2.4mm/m at ΔT 100°C) to prevent buckling.

TYPE 01 // OPEN-JOINT

Open-Joint Ventilated Rainscreen

12mm dry open shadow reveals without exposed silicone. Pressure-equalized cavity drains condensate instantly, preventing facade staining and dust streaks. Substructure: extruded 6063-T6 aluminum mullions.

TYPE 02 // SEALED-JOINT

Weatherproof Sealed Joint

Classic watertight envelope using non-staining structural silicone over PE backer rod, providing maximum air-impermeability and acoustic attenuation on galvanized steel or aluminum subframing.

TYPE 03 // HOOK-ON

High-Rise Hook-On Cassette

Modular interlocking hung brackets designed for expedited exterior erection up to 150m² per team per day. Features neoprene anti-rattle bushings with each cassette individually demountable.
