Facade Engineering Typologies // 定制选项

Fixing Methods & Substructure Details

Three engineered fixing typologies with full CAD elevation detail packages — from pressure-equalized open joints to crane-assisted high-rise cassette erection.

Open-Joint Ventilated Rainscreen (TYPE 01)

12mm dry open shadow reveals without exposed silicone; pressure-equalized cavity drains condensate instantly. Substructure: extruded 6063-T6 aluminum mullion. Best for commercial towers & public museums.

Weatherproof Sealed Joint (TYPE 02)

Non-staining structural silicone (Dow Corning 791 / Sika hybrids) over PE backer rod; maximum air-impermeability and acoustic attenuation. Best for high-acoustic zones & hospital facades.

High-Rise Hook-On Cassette (TYPE 03)

Modular interlocking hung brackets for crane-assisted erection at up to 150m² per team per day; each panel individually demountable with neoprene anti-rattle bushings. Best for supertall skyscrapers.

Facade Engineering Typologies // 安装方式

Fixing Methods & Substructure Details

Three engineered fixing typologies with CAD elevation detail packages available. All fixing bracket slots feature 6mm ovalized thermal glide tolerances (aluminum linear expansion 2.4mm/m at ΔT 100°C) to prevent buckling.

TYPE 01 // OPEN-JOINT

Open-Joint Ventilated Rainscreen

12mm dry open shadow reveals without exposed silicone. Pressure-equalized cavity drains condensate instantly, preventing facade staining and dust streaks. Joint width 10–16mm open gap on extruded 6063-T6 aluminum mullions. Best for commercial towers and public museums.

TYPE 02 // SEALED

Weatherproof Sealed Joint

Classic watertight envelope using non-staining structural silicone (Dow Corning 791 / Sika hybrids) over PE backer rod — maximum air-impermeability and acoustic attenuation on galvanized steel tube or aluminum angle substructures. Best for high-acoustic zones and hospital facades.

TYPE 03 // HOOK-ON

High-Rise Hook-On Cassette

Modular interlocking hung brackets designed for expedited crane-assisted exterior erection — up to 150m² per team per day. Neoprene anti-rattle bushings; each panel individually demountable for lifetime utility inspection. Best for supertall skyscrapers and tight urban sites.
