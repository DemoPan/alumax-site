The Product

What are Solid Aluminum Cladding Panels?

Aluviona solid aluminum cladding panels are CNC brake-formed from single-sheet monolithic alloy plate — completely eliminating composite core materials, combustible bonding adhesives, and delamination risks. Engineered for exterior rainscreen facades, monumental soffits, column covers, entrance portals, and high-traffic interior public transit hubs, our solid cassettes deliver supreme optical flatness, Class A2 fire resistance, and full demountability for lifetime building maintenance.

Customization Options

Tailored to Your Project

Every architectural project demands unique spatial geometry. We manufacture tailored solid aluminum cladding panels strictly engineered to project elevations and BIM models — encompassing custom dimensions, roll-bent curves, CNC acoustic perforations, 3D parametric cassettes, and engineered subframe brackets.

Brake-Formed Rainscreen Cassettes

Precision 4-side brake-formed box panels with mitered welded corners and factory-bonded channel stiffeners every 500mm to eliminate optical oil-canning across monumental curtain walls.

Curved & Roll-Bent Profiles

Precision roll-bent single-radius and conical cassettes delivering smooth, fluid facade transitions with continuous shadow reveals for rounded building corners, column covers, and canopies.

Architectural CNC Perforations

Custom decorative and acoustic perforation motifs engineered for solar control, facade ventilation, and dramatic night backlighting with zero structural edge distortion.

3D Parametric & Prismatic Facets

Folded geometric pyramidal and hyperbolic diamond cassettes creating dynamic sculptural chiaroscuro light-and-shadow variations across signature landmark envelopes.

Installation Typologies

Versatile Structural Mounting & Integration Options

Three engineered fixing typologies with comprehensive CAD/BIM elevation detail packages. Each system accommodates project wind-load criteria, thermal movement tolerances, and specific building movement requirements.

TYPE 01 // OPEN-JOINT

Open-Joint Ventilated Rainscreen

12mm dry open shadow reveals without exposed silicone. Pressure-equalized cavity drains condensate instantly, preventing facade staining and dust streaks. Substructure: extruded 6063-T6 aluminum mullions.

TYPE 02 // SEALED-JOINT

Weatherproof Sealed Joint

Classic watertight envelope using non-staining structural silicone over closed-cell PE backer rod, providing maximum air-impermeability and acoustic attenuation on galvanized steel tube or aluminum angle subframing.

TYPE 03 // HOOK-ON

High-Rise Hook-On Cassette

Modular interlocking hung brackets designed for expedited crane-assisted exterior erection up to 150m² per team per day. Neoprene anti-rattle bushings with each cassette individually demountable for utility maintenance.

Engineering Note: System selection and bracket spacing are determined by project wind load calculations (ASTM E330), panel thickness, and building height. All mounting brackets incorporate 6mm slotted oval holes to accommodate aluminum thermal expansion (2.4 mm/m at ΔT 100°C).
