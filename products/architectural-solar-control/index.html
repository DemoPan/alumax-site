Architectural Details // 安装方式

Subframe Typologies & Engineered Mounting Assemblies

Constructed with fully concealed fastening brackets to eliminate external bolt heads while accommodating high thermal expansion in dynamic tall envelopes.

TYPOLOGY 01 // SNAP-ON

Snap-On Anti-Vibration Clip

Interlocking extruded clip with neoprene bushing securely locks the louver blade onto vertical aluminum mullions for rapid field erection. Thermal expansion gap ±4.0mm, installation 3.2 min/m², wind flutter noise < 25 dBA.

TYPOLOGY 02 // STRINGER

Continuous Hidden Stringer Box

Concealed rear spine enables unbroken horizontal ribbon lines spanning hundreds of meters across airport and podium facades without visible break lines. Max unsupported span 2,400mm, rear deflection anchor 6061-T6 lug.

TYPOLOGY 03 // DRAINAGE

Integrated Drainage Gutter Sill

Engineered for plantroom intakes: internal blade troughs collect driven water droplets and route them through enclosed side jamb conduits into the drainage weeping tray. Runoff discharge 45 L/min per bay, 18mm S-lip trap, EPDM dual-lip sump gasket.
