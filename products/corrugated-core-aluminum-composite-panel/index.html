Systems Architecture // 系统介绍

What is the ALU-CC-4000 Corrugated Core Panel?

Unlike FR-ACP with mineral-filled polyethylene, the ALU-CC-4000 eliminates organic thermoplastic resins completely. The patented 12.5mm wave-pitch sinusoidal aluminum core acts as an infinite series of structural I-beams — 100% recyclable, zero-VOC, and EPD ISO 14025 compliant for LEED v4 / BREEAM credits.

Fabrication & Joinery Details // 节点详图

Standard Milling Profiles & Cassette Assembly

Engineered for CNC V-groove routing using standard tungsten carbide 90° or 135° cutters. Panels fold seamlessly into rigid architectural cassettes without fracturing the outer roll-coated skin.

V-Groove 90° Routing & Flange Return (DETAIL 01)

Routed from the backside through rear skin and corrugated core; a calibrated 0.2–0.3mm core base remains undisturbed to prevent metal fatigue when cold-folding 90° returns. Minimum bend radius R = 2.0mm.

Concealed Fastening Cassette Z-Clip (DETAIL 02)

Cassette folded flanges riveted with structural corner brackets; panels hook onto continuous vertical aluminum T- or Z-runners with stainless isolating washers preventing bimetallic corrosion. 50mm cavity minimum.

Horizontal Shadow Gap Express Joint (DETAIL 03)

Engineered open shadow gaps (15–20mm) with dry-joint EPDM/Neoprene multi-fin continuous weather gaskets or open-joint rainscreen baffle designs with rear weeping drainage. Expansion allowance ±3.5mm per panel.
