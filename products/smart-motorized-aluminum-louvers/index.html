The Product

What are Smart Motorized Aluminum Louvers?

The motorized aluminum louver system consists of high-performance aluminum blades, a supporting structure, a drive system, and an intelligent control system. By adjusting the blade angle in real time, it dynamically controls direct sunlight, minimizes thermal radiation, maximizes natural daylighting, and sculpts the distinct visual aesthetic of the building facade.

Installation Typologies

Versatile Structural Mounting & Integration Options

Engineered to interface seamlessly with all primary building structures, curtain wall envelopes, and architectural canopy openings.

TYPE 01 // EXTERIOR RETROFIT

Surface-Mounted

Engineered for solid exterior walls and building facade retrofits. Heavy-duty cantilever standoff bracket arms anchor directly onto structural concrete or steel substructures without requiring alterations to existing window openings.

TYPE 02 // WINDOW REVEAL

Recessed

Engineered for punched window openings and deep facade reveals. The motorized louver assembly mounts flush within the perimeter structural pocket with concealed side guide channels, maximizing natural shade depth and wind resistance.

TYPE 03 // PRIMARY GRID

Between-Structure

Engineered for large spans between structural steel columns, floor slab edges, and primary framing. Heavy pivot bearings absorb significant thermal expansion, high wind loads, and inter-story seismic building movement.

TYPE 04 // CURTAIN WALL

Curtain Wall Integrated

Engineered for unitized and stick-built glass curtain wall transoms and mullions. Features dielectric thermal-break isolators and completely concealed motor wiring within internal aluminum profile cavities.

TYPE 05 // CANOPY & SKYLIGHT

Overhead-Mounted

Engineered for building entrance canopies, covered walkway corridors, and glass skylight roofs. Louvers suspend horizontally or at designated angles using high-tensile safety hanger rods and IP66 weather-sealed actuator drives.

All mounting brackets manufactured from Grade 304 / 316 stainless steel or heavy 6063-T6 aluminum, isolated with EPDM gaskets and dielectric shims to eliminate galvanic corrosion and thermal bridging.
