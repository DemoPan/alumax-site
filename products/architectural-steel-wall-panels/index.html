What is Series ALU-ST-7000?

Heavy-duty architectural cladding for railway concourses, civic institutions and coastal high-rise crowns — where impact resilience, fire integrity and multi-storey span flatness exceed what aluminum can deliver. Cassette depth 40–120mm, single panels up to 8,000mm continuous.

Architectural Detailing // 安装方式

Engineered Mounting Substructures

Three proprietary load-bearing fixing systems accommodate high dead loads, dynamic seismic inter-story drift, and thermal movement without visible exterior fasteners.

SYS-01 // HOOK-ON

Heavy-Duty Hook-On Rainscreen

Continuous top hook receiver tabs with integral EPDM isolators — rapid progressive installation and singular panel demountability for maintenance. Joint 15–20mm open rainscreen, ±25mm seismic drift capacity.

SYS-02 // SECRET-FIX

Flush Secret-Fix Stud Anchor

Capacitor-discharge welded M8 stainless rear studs eliminate heat tint and face print-through — a monolithic, screw-free glass-flat facade with 10mm tight hairline joints. Stud pull-out load ≥ 8.2 kN per stud.

SYS-03 // SEISMIC

Seismic Inter-Story Expansion

Floor-to-floor deflection relief channels for supertall buildings, mitigating concrete slab creep and major seismic tier displacement. Joint movement horizontal ±50mm / vertical ±35mm — AAMA 501.4 / 501.6 compliant.
