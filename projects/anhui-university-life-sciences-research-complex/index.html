corporate_fare 02 // Project Overview

Academic Research Complex Envelope Integration & Clean Laboratory Glazing Protection

The Anhui University Life Sciences Research Complex (安徽大学生命科学大楼) is a benchmark architectural development located in HEFEI, ANHUI. Aluviona engineered and supplied over 5,500 m² of high-specification architectural Solid Aluminum envelope assemblies for this landmark structure.

Designed by East China Architectural Design & Research (ECADI), the envelope features high-precision 3.0mm CNC Formed AA3003-H14 panels finished in PVDF Fluoropolymer. Every component was engineered to withstand local microclimate demands, providing certified ASTM E84 Class A non-combustible life safety, dynamic windload resistance, and long-term atmospheric durability.

Through close coordination with the engineering team of Anhui University // 安徽大学, Aluviona provided end-to-end technical optimization, comprehensive factory CNC fabrication, and Site Hoist-Ready Modular Crated Delivery to ensure rapid on-site installation and flawless facade alignment.

Aseptic Vibration Mitigation & Tight Academic Construction Schedule

As a key university biological research facility, the project demanded stringent non-combustible material safety, exceptional chemical stability against experimental laboratory exhaust, and precise installation tolerances to prevent air and vapor ingress into cleanroom zones.
